What is a 3-2-1 buydown?

It is a temporary payment subsidy that steps down over the first 3 years. It does not permanently reduce the mortgage note rate. The full contractual payment returns after the subsidy period. Availability, funding, eligibility and seller-contribution rules depend on the lender and program.

This concept does not quote rates, payments or savings, accept applications, or determine whether anyone qualifies.

What would a finished version include?

A lender-reviewed calculator, property-specific illustrations, approved disclosures, and a clear next step for an interested buyer or seller. We would confirm the scope, licensing and compliance requirements before consumer use.
