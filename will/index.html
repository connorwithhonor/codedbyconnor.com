What is a 3-2-1 buydown?

It is a temporary payment subsidy that steps down over the first 3 years. It does not permanently reduce the mortgage note rate. The full contractual payment returns after the subsidy period. Availability, funding, eligibility and seller-contribution rules depend on the lender and program.

This concept does not supply market rates, accept applications, or determine whether anyone qualifies. The calculator uses only your own scenario inputs.

What would a finished version include?

A lender-reviewed calculator, property-specific illustrations, approved disclosures, and a clear next step for an interested buyer or seller. We would confirm the scope, licensing and compliance requirements before consumer use.

The interactive calculator below works on your phone, tablet or computer. It is a demonstration tool, not an approved lending product or a personalized quote.

Important limits and how the math works

The contractual note rate does not change with a temporary buydown. The subsidy supplements the borrower’s payment. The borrower remains responsible for the contractual payment if subsidy funds are unavailable.

Payment equivalents use the original balance and original fixed term at a rate reduced by the selected plan’s percentage-point schedule. The subsidy totals each month’s difference from the full contractual payment. Final lender rounding and agreement terms may produce a slightly different amount.

For Fannie Mae loans, qualification uses the full note rate. Lender and program rules determine eligibility, allowed funding sources, contribution limits and treatment of unused funds. No future refinance, approval or income increase is assumed.
